Lifewrought

What hath life wrought?

A life is in progress

Prologue

You are about to live a whole life on Krynn, from birth to the first day of adventure. The dice decide what happens; you decide how to respond to it. Every table is shown before it is rolled, with the reasons its odds look the way they do. In pure fate, nothing on the character sheet is chosen directly: your class, your background and the rest grow out of what you lived. There is no death and no going back on a decision; whether the dice can be bent a little is up to you, below.

381 AC

The game master sets the campaign. A full life takes about 20 to 25 minutes, a quick one about 13; both are saved as you go.
